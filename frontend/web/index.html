What a finished report contains

Every successful run opens the same packet structure — know the map before you run.

01 Summary chips

Trend, News, Confidence, Risk, and Projected move — orient fast, then dig into notes.

02 Company overview

Issuer name, sector / industry context, and a short company summary when available.

03 Price & forecast

History plus a short forward path. Range pills (1M–MAX) and Reset for the chart view.

04 Four specialists

Performance, Market, Financial, and Risk notes — each with labels you can verify on-page.

05 Research brief

Stitches the four specialist notes into one memo with locked Stance, Confidence, bull/bear cases.

06 Sources

Headlines behind the Market expert note. Open links to check claims yourself.

07 On your desk

Visit-only tray of finished tickers. Compare while another run is still going. Clears on refresh.

What it shows

Solid line = observed closes. Dashed line = forecast. Range pills: 1M, 6M, 1Y, 5Y, MAX. Reset restores the view.

How to use

  • Start with 1Y, then narrow if needed
  • Confirm every specialist claim against this chart
  • Low confidence ⇒ lighter weight on the path
  • Agents must not invent different prices than the chart
